# Real Estate Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
The Real Estate Sales Dashboard is a data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze property sales data and identify important trends in the real estate market.

The dashboard converts raw property transaction data into interactive visual insights that help investors, analysts, and real estate professionals understand pricing patterns, property characteristics, and profitability drivers.

It highlights sales performance, property features, market distribution, and seasonal trends using different visualizations such as KPI cards, bar charts, scatter plots, and decomposition trees.

---

## Problem Statement
The real estate market generates a large amount of property transaction data including property prices, house size, number of bedrooms, location features, and sale dates. However, this data is difficult to interpret in its raw format.

Real estate investors, brokers, and analysts often struggle to determine which property characteristics significantly influence property prices and profitability. Understanding how factors such as property size, bedroom count, property grade, and waterfront availability affect pricing is important for making informed investment decisions.

Therefore, analyzing real estate transaction data is necessary to identify pricing trends, important value drivers, and overall market behavior.

## Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Average Price
- Price per Square Foot
- Assumed Cost
- Profit
- ROI

### Visualizations Used
- Bar Charts – Price comparison by bedrooms
- Scatter Plot – Relationship between property size and price
- Line Chart – Monthly price trends
- Donut Chart – Waterfront property distribution
- Bar Chart – Units sold by price range
- Decomposition Tree – Price drivers such as bedrooms, grade, and view

---

## Key Insights
- The real estate market generated over $12B in total sales, indicating strong market activity.
- The average property price is approximately $540K, showing strong demand for mid-range housing.
- Properties with 7–8 bedrooms reach peak pricing, after which price growth slows.
- Waterfront properties represent less than 1% of listings but contribute significantly to high-value transactions.
- Most properties sold fall within the $200K – $600K price range, dominating the housing market.
- Property grade and view quality strongly influence house prices.
- Price trends show seasonal fluctuations indicating possible buying patterns during certain months.
